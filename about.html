About us

An engineer who got tired of watching people get overcharged.

San Diego Spray Foam is family owned and operated. When you call, you get the people who will be standing in your attic.

The founder

Vlad Gordievsky

Vlad is an MIT-educated mechanical engineer and a father of two. He started San Diego Spray Foam in 2022 after seeing how a previous employer in the home insulation industry priced its work — and deciding he could do the same job honestly and still make a living at it.

Before any of that, he did what a lot of engineers do with a problem: he experimented on his own house. He tried different techniques and materials on his own Colonial home before he ever sold the service, which is where the interest in building science stopped being theoretical.

That is still how the company runs. We specialise in spray foam, but we assess each project on its own terms and recommend the most appropriate solution and materials for the job in front of us — even when that means telling you the expensive option is not the one you need.

What we are committed to

Trust, craftsmanship, service

Three words that get printed on a lot of contractor vans. Here is what we actually mean by them.

High standards of trust

An honest quote with no hidden costs, competitively priced, and a straight recommendation even when it is smaller than the one you asked for. Integrity and transparency are not a differentiator to us — they are the baseline.

Superior craftsmanship

Top-quality insulation tailored to your home. That is masking done properly, consistent lift thickness, foam trimmed where it needs to be flush, and a site left cleaner than we found it.

Exceptional service

From consultation to completion. We listen to the concerns you raise, walk you through the solutions rather than talking over you, and finish with five-star attention to the details and the cleanup.

Our process

What working with us looks like

  1. Consultation

    We begin with a free at-home insulation quote on site. We discuss your needs and explore the options with you before anyone quotes anything.

  2. Quote design

    An honest quote with no hidden costs. We work hard to stay competitively priced and to show you what each line actually buys.

  3. Timeline

    Projects can typically be quoted and completed within a month’s time, if not sooner.

  4. On-site engagement

    We listen to your concerns and walk through the solution with you before and during the work. No surprises appearing behind the masking.

  5. Quality finishing

    Five-star service means meticulous attention to detail and a professional cleanup. We are not finished until the site is.

The crew

Small team, on purpose

Vlad and his team specialise in spray foam but handle the full range of home insulation work. Staying small is what lets us assess every project individually instead of running a script — and it is why the person who quotes your job is the person who shows up to do it.

It also means we can be honest about capacity. If a job is not a fit, or the timeline you need is not one we can hold, we will tell you that on the first call rather than three weeks in.
